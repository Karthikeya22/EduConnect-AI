# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Teachers looking to grade student submissions from Canvas. They are in a focused, high-cognitive-load context where they need to process multiple assignments quickly, accurately, and fairly.

## Product Purpose
A Canvas Grading AI assistant. It accepts a Canvas API key to retrieve courses as "hubs." Within these hubs, teachers can view students, grades, assignments, and submissions. The core feature is a RAG-based AI system that suggests grades and provides detailed explanations, enabling teachers to grade faster, with less friction and higher consistency.

## Brand Personality
Clean, focused, and visually stunning but uncluttered. The UI should fade into the background so teachers can focus entirely on their work.

## Anti-references
"AI slop" (overly saturated gradients, decorative glassmorphism everywhere, generic "cream/sand" backgrounds, identical card grids, and marketing buzzwords). The design must feel highly crafted, deliberate, and free of typical AI generator tropes.

## Design Principles
- **Data over decoration**: Information density should serve the teacher's workflow; keep essential actions within reach without adding visual noise.
- **Intentional precision**: Avoid boilerplate aesthetics. Use deliberate, refined color combinations that enhance focus rather than distract.
- **Effortless workflow**: The tool should feel like an extension of the teacher's thought process—calm, reliable, and deeply frictionless.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Standard web accessibility practices. No specific extraordinary requirements, but contrast and readability must be excellent to support long grading sessions.
